Controls

Keys can be changed in Settings → CONTROLS. Enter and Esc cannot: they are the way out of whatever they open.

PC: moving and acting

KeyAction
W A S Dmove
Mouselook (click the window to capture the cursor)
Spacejump (costs stamina); in water, hold to swim up
Left Shiftrun (costs stamina); forward and diagonally forward only
Left mouse (hold)break a block; hit an animal or player where you look
Right mouseplace a block; use what is in your hand (light a fire with flint, till with a hoe, open a hearth or chest, drink water with an empty hand)
Shift + right mouseset one item down on top of a block (a knife on a stone, an ingot on the floor); with a log, start a log pile; next update at a butcher's table with a bronze cleaver in hand, drive the cleaver into the table, and again to take it out
Alt + right mouseplace one layer of loose material instead of a whole block
Left Alt (hold)look around without turning the body
1–9, 0, mouse wheelchoose a belt slot
Epick up the thing you are looking at: something lying on the ground, or an arrow stuck in a tree. Nothing is picked up by walking over it
Feat what is in the selected slot
Qtap: drop one at your feet (Shift+Q: the whole stack). next update hold and release: throw it, further the longer you hold
V (hold)next update raise a shield from the belt
Rgive up while downed / respawn

Tools never stack: one axe, knife, pick or hoe per slot.

Riding

Right-click a tame horse to mount. W trot, W+Shift gallop, W+C walk, S rein in or back up, A/D turn, Space jump, C dismount when the horse stands. C + right-click opens the saddlebags.

Raft

Standing on the deck, right-click the raft to take the oars; W/S row, A/D turn, right-click again to raise or stow the sail, Space to stand up.
