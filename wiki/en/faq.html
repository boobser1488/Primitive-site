Questions players ask

Short answers, with a link to the long one.

Where is the hoe? How do I farm?

The hoe is made by hand: 2 flint flakes + 1 worked stick + 3 fibre (or 2 bones instead of the flakes). Right-click grass or earth with it to make farmland, then place seeds on it. Seeds come from wild wheat, mostly in the steppe; grass does not drop them. In version 1.5.2 a right-click with the hoe could do nothing at all; that is a bug, fixed in the next update. Farming.

How do I open the recipes?

Press B, or the RECIPES button in the lower right corner of the pack (I). On a phone there is a RECIPES button on the glass, top right next to "...", from the first minute next update. The book only shows what you know: a recipe is learned once every ingredient has been in your pack. One missing ingredient shows as ?. The right side of the pack's PACK tab lists what you can make right now. Crafting.

I have copper. What now?

Two roads, and both are a journey. Bronze: find a tin district, usually 400–800 blocks from where you started; stream tin lies on its river banks. 4 copper + 1 tin at the kiln make 5 bronze. Iron: with a copper pickaxe, dig deep for iron ore, or gather rusty stones along rivers and bogs; build a bloomery (16 bricks + 4 mortar + 8 cobblestone) and burn lots of charcoal. Steel, made from iron at the kiln, beats both. The path.

What should I be doing next?

Open the pack and its LADDER tab. It opens on the first age you have not reached, lists what you have never held and says what kind of place it is in.

My stone axe will not cut down a tree.

A freshly lashed axe only cuts brush, and a lashed pickaxe will not take stone. Wedge it: the tool + 2 pegs, by hand (a worked stick makes 4 pegs). First tools.

How do I make fire without stones?

Drop three sticks and one log on the same spot with Q, then right-click the ground under them with flint in your hand. Fire.

My copper will not melt.

Copper needs orange heat, and only charcoal as the fuel gives it. Make charcoal first: 3 logs in a fire make 1. The crucible and mould must be fired. The line under the fire says what heat is missing.

Where is clay?

Under low, flat ground near water: floodplains, old lake beds, low meadows. Dig a few blocks down, or look at a cut river bank, where it shows as a layer between earth and stone.

M does nothing.

You need a map in your pack: 3 leather + 1 charcoal by hand. It draws only where you walk while carrying it. Finding your way.

Where do I get leather?

A hide from a carcass (cut with a knife) stretched on a hide frame (4 sticks + 2 fibre) becomes leather by itself over time. Fresh hides can also be sewn straight into a fur hood and cape.

The water made me sick.

Pond and lake water makes you ill two times in five, sea water always makes thirst worse. Drink from running water, or boil a jug of pond water in a burning hearth for twenty seconds.

My fire went out.

Rain puts out fires in the open within a minute. Put one block directly above it. Fires also burn out: a campfire holds at most twenty minutes of fuel.

I died. Are my things gone?

No: they are in your body where you fell. Go back within two days; after that the soft things (food, leather, cloth, fibre) rot away and metal, stone and wood remain.

Why can I not see in the dark?

Moonless nights are meant to be dark. Make a torch (stick + fibre) and light it at a fire. If darkness keeps you from playing at all, ambient_light in client_settings.toml raises it.

The game runs slowly.

Lower RENDER DISTANCE in the settings: it is the strongest lever. Simple terrain from is the second. Shadows are off by default and cost frame time when turned on. F3 shows where the frame goes.

Is there a creative mode?

No separate mode. In a world with COMMANDS AND TESTING on, the G menu gives you any item, and /time, /weather and /tp work. The TEST world type has every station, crop and animal built near the spawn.
