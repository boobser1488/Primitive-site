Field notes

A player's guide to Primitive: stone age to iron age, alone or on a small server.

You wake with nothing, break a rock with your hands and work up through flint, clay, copper, bronze and iron. Every step is a reason to go somewhere: you leave the meadow because the ore is in the hills, and you come back because your field and your fire are at home.

This guide was written from the game's own code and illustrated with frames taken in the game. Where this guide and the game disagree, trust the game.

  • Getting startedA new world, the first stone, flint, tools, fire, water and food.
  • The pathSeven ages from bare hands to iron, and where to go for each.
  • Crafting and stationsThe recipe book, hearths and heat, the kiln, the pit kiln.
  • FarmingSeeds, the hoe, water, frost, ash and bread.
  • Survival and the bodyHunger, thirst, cold, wet wool, wounds, weight, death.
  • Animals and huntingWhat is dangerous, tracks, spears and bows, sheep and horses.
  • The world and the mapThe planet, the land, signs in the landscape, finding your way.
  • ControlsEvery key on PC and every button on Android.
  • MultiplayerJoining a server and running your own.
  • QuestionsWhere is the hoe? How do I open recipes? What after copper?

Which version. These notes follow the current build. Things marked next update are not yet in version 1.5.2 on itch.io and arrive with the next release.
